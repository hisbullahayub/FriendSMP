Server rules

Play hard, play fair. Staff decisions are final.

#1 Hacked clients & unfair advantages

KillAura, Fly, Speed, Baritone, Auto-Totem, free-cam and similar mods are banned.

Punishment: 30-day ban, then permanent blacklist

#2 Combat logging

Disconnecting in combat kills you and drops your inventory.

Punishment: Combat death, inventory drop, 1-hour temp ban

#3 Duplication & glitch exploiting

Do not exploit bugs or redstone dupes. Report them to staff.

Punishment: Inventory wipe and permanent ban

#4 Toxicity, harassment & hate speech

No racism, hate speech, doxxing threats or advertising other servers.

Punishment: 1-day mute, 7-day ban, permanent ban
